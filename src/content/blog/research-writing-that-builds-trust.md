Research-based writing stands out when it turns complex information into clear decisions.

## Begin with the reader question

Before drafting, define one core reader problem. Every section should help answer that question.

## Use sources strategically

You do not need ten references per paragraph. You need the right references in the right place.

- Use high-quality primary sources first.
- Summarize findings in plain language.
- Link claims to evidence without overloading the reader.

## Build a predictable structure

A reliable structure reduces cognitive load and keeps readers engaged.

1. Context.
2. Evidence.
3. Recommendation.

This pattern works for articles, reports, and long-form blog posts.

## End with a practical takeaway

The best research writing helps readers act. Close with clear next steps, not vague conclusions.
