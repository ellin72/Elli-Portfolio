A strong CV is not about listing every task you have ever done. It is about proving value quickly.

## Start with impact, not responsibilities

Recruiters scan fast. Your bullets should begin with outcomes and measurable impact where possible.

- Improved page load speed by 32% by optimizing image delivery and code splitting.
- Reduced onboarding time by documenting deployment steps and templates.
- Increased lead conversion by redesigning landing page content and call-to-action flow.

## Use a role-specific profile summary

Your summary should communicate three things:

1. Your current focus.
2. Your strongest domain.
3. The business value you bring.

Keep it to three or four lines and avoid generic phrases.

## Keep formatting recruiter-friendly

Use clear sections, consistent spacing, and short bullets. A clean format improves readability and signals professionalism.

> Think of your CV as a product page for your skills.

## Final checklist

- Is every bullet outcome-oriented?
- Are tools and skills grouped logically?
- Can someone understand your strengths in less than one minute?

When those answers are yes, your CV is ready.
