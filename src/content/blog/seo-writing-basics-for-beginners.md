Good SEO writing serves two audiences at once: readers and search engines.

## Choose one primary keyword

Select one phrase that matches user intent. Keep your article focused around that phrase and related variations.

## Place keywords naturally

Avoid repetition. Include the keyword where it helps comprehension:

- Title
- Intro paragraph
- One subheading
- Meta description

## Optimize readability first

Readable content performs better over time. Use short paragraphs, descriptive headings, and direct language.

## Add value beyond optimization

Search-friendly content still needs substance. Include examples, checklists, or steps readers can apply immediately.

When your content is useful, clarity and rankings improve together.
